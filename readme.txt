controls
numpad to move

i to use items
g to pick up said items
d to drop them
you can attack enemies and destroy walls by breaking into them
press \ to force attack
most enemies don't attack right away
press a to pull up you ability list
press enter on any menu to use it and backspace to cancel it

if you want to use the level editor you can press f1 to use it and press it 
again to play as normal

level editor controls

by default you are stuck in the player's pov press the toggle camera button to go to editor cam
from there you can press wasd to move and zoom and pan with middle mouse button
you can click on the different buttons to do different things they are all labeled
